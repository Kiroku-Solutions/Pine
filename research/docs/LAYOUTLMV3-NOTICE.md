# LayoutLMv3 attribution and license

**Original model:** [LayoutLMv3 Base (`microsoft/layoutlmv3-base`)](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/layoutlmv3-base), published by Microsoft. Credit to Yupan Huang, Tengchao Lv, Lei Cui, Yutong Lu, and Furu Wei, authors of [LayoutLMv3: Pre-training for Document AI with Unified Text and Image Masking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.08387) (ACM Multimedia, 2022).

**Pine adaptation:** The Pine team fine-tuned the original model for receipt field extraction, exported it to ONNX, and produced INT8 variants, including a variant with quantized embeddings.
